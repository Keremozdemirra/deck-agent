## Preconditions, filled in before writing anything

- **Decision.** Board approves €24m capex in the 2027 plan and mandates PPA negotiation
  to close by Q1 2027 — or does not, and the company continues buying certificates.
- **Decider.** The board, effectively the two independent members who chair the audit
  and investment committees.
- **Counter-position.** Finance's position, already circulated: keep buying certificates,
  revisit in 2028 when the ETS price path is clearer. Doing nothing is the default.
- **Time.** 40 minutes, of which the CFO expects 15 minutes of questions. Repository
  convention of two minutes per slide gives a budget of roughly 12 slides.

Note what the brief does **not** ask for and does not get: a CBAM explainer, an ESG
strategy chapter, a competitor benchmark. The board approved the CBAM readiness
programme in 2024; the situation is not in dispute.
