# Marmara Alüminyum — board deck, 8 September — storyline

Decision: approve €24m for electrified remelting in the 2027 plan and mandate PPA
closure by Q1 2027.
Decider: the board, in practice the two independent members.
Counter-position: Finance's paper — keep buying certificates, revisit in 2028.
Time: 40 minutes, ~15 for questions. Budget: 12 slides.

All figures below are **illustrative placeholders**. Every one carries the source that
must replace it before the deck is built. Nothing here is a real Marmara number.

## Governing sentence

Marmara should commit €24m to electrified remelting in the 2027 plan rather than continue
buying CBAM certificates, because under every ETS price path we modelled the cumulative
certificate bill overtakes the investment before 2032.

One subject the board controls, one finite verb, one *because*. The earlier draft carried
two reasons joined by *and* — see the rejected list.

## Opening (S-C-Q-A)

**S** — Sixty per cent of our tonnage and about three-quarters of our gross margin is
sold into the EU. *[illustrative — 2025 product-level export ledger, Finance]*

**C** — Since 1 January 2026 those sales require the surrender of CBAM certificates
against embedded emissions, and our embedded emissions per tonne are roughly double those
of an EU-based extruder buying low-carbon billet. *(Regulation (EU) 2023/956; the
transitional reporting-only period ran 1 October 2023 to 31 December 2025. Emissions
comparison: illustrative — Marmara 2025 verified inventory vs. published EU sector
benchmark, HSE to confirm.)*

**Q** — Do we pay that gap indefinitely, or remove it?

**A** — *governing sentence, verbatim.*

The complication carries two clauses on purpose: it is the only place in the deck where a
compound sentence is allowed, because S-C-Q-A is spoken, not shown. It is not a slide.

## Appendix candidates

- Competitor electrification announcements, EU extruders, 2024–2026.
- CSRD / Scope 3 reporting implications of the same investment.
- Model documentation: ETS price paths, free-allocation phase-out assumptions, discount
  rate, and the treatment of the mass-based de minimis threshold — **confirm the current
  consolidated text of Regulation (EU) 2023/956 before quoting any threshold**.
- Vendor quotation detail and lead-time assumptions.

Delivery language is Turkish. When the headlines are rendered, each must keep a finite
verb rather than collapsing into a noun phrase — a nominalised headline states a topic,
not a claim, and would fail the Assertion test in the language the board actually hears.

## What the method changed

Read aloud, the first draft ran: overview, what CBAM is, our emissions, the technology,
the investment, the ask. Six of those are furniture or background. It contained no
sentence the board could disagree with until slide five, and no comparison against the
option Finance had already put in writing — the deck argued for something without
arguing against the alternative. The rejection pass removed eight slides' worth of
material and surfaced the missing sentence: number 8, the direct comparison, which nobody
had written because it lived across two tabs of the model.
